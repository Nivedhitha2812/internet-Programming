Vegetable Pasta

Preparation Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 200g Pasta
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 Onion
  • 100g Mixed Vegetables
  • Salt and Pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta.
  2. Chop the vegetables.
  3. Cook vegetables in a pan.
  4. Add pasta and seasoning.
  5. Serve hot.
